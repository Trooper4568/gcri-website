📜 Community Rules

  • Be respectful to everyone.
  • No bullying, harassment, or targeted attacks.
  • Keep content appropriate for the community.
  • Do not share personal information.
  • No scams, harmful links, or illegal activities.
  • Do not pretend to be school staff.
  • Follow the rules of the platform used by GCRI.
  • Moderators may remove content or members who break the rules.
